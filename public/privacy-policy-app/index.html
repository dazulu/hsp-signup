3. How the booking works

When you tap Book, your HSP email and password are transmitted over an encrypted TLS connection to a serverless function hosted on Netlify infrastructure in the United States. Before forwarding them to the next stage, the Netlify function encrypts the credentials with AES-256-GCM using a secret key that never leaves the server. The encrypted payload is then passed to a GitHub Actions workflow, also running on US-based infrastructure.

The workflow decrypts the credentials using the server-held key, immediately masks them so they cannot appear in any log output, and uses them only to run a browser automation script that logs in to the Hochschulsport Hamburg website on your behalf and completes the booking.

In summary: credentials leave your device over TLS, are re-encrypted with AES-256-GCM before crossing to GitHub, are decrypted only at the point of use, and are never written to any server storage. Your password does not appear in any execution logs.

4. Photo likes

When you like a photo in a gallery, the App generates a random pseudonymous ID (UUID) that is stored on your device. This ID — together with the photo identifier — is sent to a serverless function on Netlify and stored in Netlify Blobs so that your likes persist and like counts are accurate. Although this ID is not connected to your name, email address, or any directly identifying information, it constitutes pseudonymous personal data under GDPR because it is linkable to your device.

You can delete your likes data at any time from Settings → Delete my likes data in the app. This removes all server-side records associated with your pseudonymous ID and resets the ID on your device.

5. Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Art. 6(1)(b) — Performance of a contract / pre-contractual steps: When you initiate a booking, we process your HSP credentials solely to carry out the action you have explicitly requested — completing your Hochschulsport Hamburg session booking on your behalf. Processing is limited to what is strictly necessary to fulfil that request.
  • Art. 6(1)(a) — Consent: If you enable "Remember login details", you actively opt in to storing your credentials in encrypted device storage for future convenience. You can withdraw this consent at any time by disabling the toggle, which immediately deletes the stored credentials from your device.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest: Photo likes use a pseudonymous device ID to provide the feature you interact with. The impact on your privacy is minimal because the ID cannot directly identify you. You can delete your likes data at any time via Settings.

6. On-device storage (§ 25 TDDDG)

The App stores the following data on your device:

  • Native app: HSP credentials optionally saved in encrypted device storage (Android Keystore-backed hardware encryption) — only if you enable "Remember login details". Sport choice, last booking timestamp, and language preference in AsyncStorage. Pseudonymous device ID in encrypted device storage.
  • Web: Sport choice, last booking timestamp, and pseudonymous device ID in localStorage. Credentials are held in browser memory only and are never written to storage.

This storage is necessary to provide the functionality you request (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

7. Data sharing & international transfers

Your credentials are transmitted to:

  • Netlify (serverless function host & blob storage for likes, US — EU-US Data Privacy Framework & Standard Contractual Clauses)
  • GitHub Actions (workflow runner, US — EU-US Data Privacy Framework & Standard Contractual Clauses)
  • Hochschulsport Hamburg (the booking target)
  • Expo Updates (over-the-air app update checks, US — EU-US Data Privacy Framework). No personal data is sent; only the app version is checked.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third party.

8. Data retention

  • Credentials on the native app: stored until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Credentials on the web: exist only in browser memory and are discarded when the page is closed.
  • Credentials in transit: not persisted on any server. Workflow logs do not contain your password (it is masked immediately after decryption using GitHub's log-masking feature). GitHub Actions workflow run logs are retained for 7 days and then automatically deleted. Playwright report and auth-failure artifacts uploaded by this workflow are also set to 4-day retention and are deleted after that.
  • Photo likes: stored server-side until you delete them via Settings, or until the club removes them. The pseudonymous device ID on your device persists until you delete your likes data, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Art. 15)
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erase your data (Art. 17)
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest at any time (Art. 21) — applies to photo likes
  • Withdraw consent for "Remember login details" at any time by disabling the toggle in the app (Art. 7(3))

To erase your server-side likes data, go to Settings → Delete my likes data in the app. This also resets your pseudonymous device ID. For credentials, disable "Remember login details" or uninstall the app. To make a formal request or exercise any other right, email info@hamburggaa.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hamburg data protection authority (Hamburgischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
